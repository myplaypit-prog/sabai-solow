## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intentional sanctuary for solo travelers aged 30 to 50 exploring the tranquil, lesser-known towns of Thailand—from Pai and Nan to Chiang Khan and Koh Kood. The name echoes the Thai philosophy of *Sabai* (comfort, ease, lighthearted peacefulness) merged with independent discovery (*Solo*).

The emotional target is **effortless reassurance coupled with understated wonder**. The audience seeks escape from demanding professional schedules without navigating chaotic backpacker spaces or sterile corporate package tour portals. They require immediate legibility, high visual confidence, generous touch real estate, and a warm, sun-warmed editorial charm.

The visual style blends **Editorial Bento Modernism** with **Warm Tactile Organic Surfaces**:
- High-contrast, publication-grade typography grounded in deep marine indigo gives authoritative structure.
- Generous, breathable canvas layouts evoke textured linen paper rather than glaring digital screens.
- Organic fluid geometry (nested rounded curves, floating action pills) introduces tactile friendliness without drifting into juvenile playfulness.
- Utility takes precedence: minimum 44px hit-targets, explicit safety badges, and calm glanceable metadata reduce cognitive friction under bright tropical sunlight.

## Colors

The color system draws from authentic Thai landscapes: terra cotta temple tiles, deep Andaman waters, lush monsoon palms, and golden street-side mangoes. The default mode is intentionally warm-light to simulate premium matte print stationery.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#E05A2B` - Saffron Sunset):** Drives core calls to action, highlighted reservation triggers, active day markers, and primary progress indicators. Vibrancy is counter-balanced by warm earth undertones.
- **Secondary (`#1B2A4A` - Siam Marine Indigo):** Provides structural authority, crisp typographic contrast, navigation headers, and stabilizing base containers.
- **Tertiary (`#2A7258` - Tropical Palm Sage):** Handles nature/sustainability markers, solo safety certifications ('안심 코스'), and positive availability states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Linen / `#F4EFE6` - Raw Oat):** Forms the foundational background, preventing glare and providing eye comfort for prolonged reading.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for interactive cards, bento modules, and popovers to create clean lift against the warm linen canvas.
- **Accent Highlight (`#F5B738` - Golden Mango):** Used strictly for cultural highlights, festival micro-tags, and solo ratings ('혼행 적합도 5.0').
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text rests at `#1B2A4A` (exceeding WCAG AAA on `#FFFFFF` and `#FAF7F2`). Secondary copy sits at `#53637F`. Muted borders utilize `#E8DFD3`.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes high legibility and refined contemporary travel journalism. When serving Korean copy in production environments, the engine maps seamlessly to **Pretendard**, paired internationally with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for Latin characters and numerals.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Punchy, high-impact, tight negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em). Creates an editorial poster feel for town introductions (e.g., "치앙마이 너머, 조용한 치앙다운의 밤").
- **Body Text:** No critical body copy ever drops below 16px (`body-md`), ensuring swift, comfortable reading without visual strain during mobile navigation. Paragraphs use relaxed line-heights (1.6 to 1.65) to maximize eye track retention.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-copy (`label-sm`, `label-md`) preserves structural clarity via semi-bold and bold weights with balanced positive tracking, guaranteeing that metadata like solo safety ratings and budget indicators remain sharp across all resolutions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces asymmetric Bento and editorial modular grids. Content feels intentional, uncrowded, and structured for stress-free exploration.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layouts utilizing `margin: 1.25rem` (20px). Cards stretch edge-to-edge within boundaries. Multi-item horizontal carousels bleed cleanly off-screen with snap-alignment to encourage natural thumb swiping.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column grid with `gutter: 1.5rem` and `margin: 2rem`. Modules begin organizing into Bento clusters (e.g., solo travel index next to local transit guides).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column adaptive grid capped at a maximum container width of `1280px` centered within generous canvas margins (`margin-desktop: 3rem`).

### Rhythm Rules
- Internal card padding strictly matches `space-md` (20px) on mobile and expands to `space-lg` (32px) on desktop hero units.
- Vertical section pacing utilizes `space-xl` (56px) to cleanly separate daily itineraries from village neighborhood overviews, preventing data saturation.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids cold, artificial drop-shadows. Depth is articulated through **Warm Ambient Diffusions** and **Subtle Organic Borders**, recreating the tactile feeling of stacked linen stock and travel sketchbooks.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Background surface in `#FAF7F2`. Completely flat with no shadow.
- **Card Surface (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surface framed by a hairline border (`1px solid #E8DFD3`). Paired with a soft sunlit tint shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(224, 90, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Hover & Drag (Level 2):** Subtle upward translation (`translateY(-3px)`) with heightened shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.09), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(224, 90, 43, 0.06)`. Border color warms to `#D8CCBD`.
- **Floating Modals & Pinned Navigation (Level 3):** Floating bottom action bars and sticky booking trays use a frosted translucent warm layer (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), bounded by `1px solid rgba(232, 223, 211, 0.8)` and grounded by `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2**, striking a balance between modern tech polish and gentle, organic Thai craftwork.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Small Badges):** `rounded-md` (8px to 12px) ensures structural precision and high touch accuracy.
- **Primary Content Cards & Bento Blocks:** `rounded-lg` (16px) to `rounded-xl` (24px) for a welcoming, pillowed perimeter that draws focus inwards.
- **Floating Controls, Badges & Micro-tags:** Full organic pill curves (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for chips, status tags, and floating action triggers.
- **Image Containers:** Paired with matching inner card radii minus 4px to produce balanced concentric borders.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Saffron Sunset):** Background `#E05A2B`, text `#FFFFFF`, minimum height `52px` (mobile touch priority), padding `0 28px`, font `label-lg`. Active state shifts to `#C84E23`. Micro-shadow: `0 4px 14px rgba(224, 90, 43, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary (Marine Outline):** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #1B2A4A`, text `#1B2A4A`, `rounded-full`. Hover adds subtle cream fill `#F4EFE6`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary (Linen Calm):** Background `#F4EFE6`, text `#1B2A4A`. Effortless, low-friction secondary actions.

### 2. Micro-tags & Status Badges
Always formatted as pills (`rounded-full`) with a distinct icon and concise copy:
- **Solo Safety Tag ('안심 코스'):** Background `#EAF3F0`, text `#2A7258`, emerald shield icon.
- **Solo Fit Rating ('혼행 적합도 5.0'):** Background `#FEF7EA`, text `#B27B08`, solid golden star icon.
- **Local Alert ('금주일 알림 / 휴무'):** Background `#FDEEE9`, text `#D1431A`, subtle pulse dot.
- **Budget Badge ('1박 10만원 이하'):** Background `#F0F4FA`, text `#243B6B`.

### 3. Bento Itinerary Cards
- Pure white container with `20px` radius, hairline border `#E8DFD3`, and `20px` internal padding.
- Displays sequential day timeline markers with terra cotta connecting threads.
- Direct metadata cluster: Solo safety index, average transit ease, and walking distance displayed via standardized icons.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Selectors
- Minimum height `52px` for comfortable finger tapping.
- Background `#FAF7F2` in resting state; transitions to crisp `#FFFFFF` with `#E05A2B` border focus ring (`2px`).
- Floating placeholder labels utilizing `label-md` in secondary slate `#53637F`.

### 5. Checkboxes & Filter Radios
- Size `24px x 24px` to avoid misclicks.
- Selected state fills with `#1B2A4A` (Indo-navy) featuring a clean white checkmark, moving away from generic blue styles.

### 6. Floating Solo Navigation Bar (Mobile)
- Docked `16px` above the bottom screen margin, spanning centered width with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`.
- Encloses 4 key safe-navigation points: Explore, My Plan, Safe Spot, Solo Community.
- Active states feature Saffron dot accents beneath the primary icon.
